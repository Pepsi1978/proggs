Delete Your Best Journal Account and Data

App: Best Journal (Android)
Publisher: Frank Barwandt
Last updated: 20 April 2026

Option 1: Delete directly in the app (recommended)

  1. Open Best Journal on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (bottom-right of the main screen).
  3. Scroll to Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm twice. All your data will be deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app (for example, because you uninstalled it or lost your device), send us an email. We will delete your account within 14 days.

Send an email to: dev.app.support@gmail.com

Subject line: Delete my Best Journal account

Include: the email address you used to sign in (so we can identify your account).

Compose deletion email

What gets deleted

Data categoryStatus after deletion
Firebase Authentication account (email, user ID)Permanently deleted
All journal entries (text, images, audio, videos)Permanently deleted
Dashboard summaries and AI-generated reviewsPermanently deleted
Firebase Analytics instance IDDissociated within 14 months (Google default)
Google Drive backup folder (app-private)Dissociated; you can manually delete it in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps
Google Play subscription historyRetained by Google Play for statutory billing records (7 years, German tax law)

Retention for legitimate interests

The following data may be retained for the period indicated, as required by law or for fraud prevention:

  • Google Play billing records: 7 years (German §147 AO tax law) — handled by Google, not by us
  • Firebase Analytics aggregate statistics: Anonymized after 14 months (only if you opted in)

Personal journal content is never retained after account deletion.

Response time

If you use Option 2 (email), we aim to confirm and complete your deletion request within 14 days on business days. For urgent cases, mark the subject line with "URGENT".

Your data protection rights

In addition to deletion, you have the following rights under the EU GDPR and comparable laws:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

For more information, see our full Privacy Policy or read it in the app under Settings → Privacy.
