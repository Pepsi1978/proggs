## 2. Description of the App

Best Journal is a digital diary with AI-powered features. The main functions include:

- Creating and managing journal entries (text, voice recording, photos, videos)
- Speech-to-text transcription (locally on your device or via external cloud partners)
- AI-powered summaries, dashboard analyses and reviews
- Text improvement by AI
- Text-to-speech read-aloud feature
- Optional cloud backup via Google Drive
- PDF export of your entries
