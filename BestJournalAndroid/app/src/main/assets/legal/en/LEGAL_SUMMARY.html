What data does the app process?

Best Journal is a diary app. Your journal entries stay locally on your device by default. Data only leaves your device if you actively enable an optional feature:

  • Cloud voice transcription (Groq, USA) — optional
  • Cloud backup (Google Drive app folder) — optional
  • AI features (Firebase AI / Google Gemini) — optional
  • Text-to-speech (Microsoft Edge TTS) — optional
  • Sign-in (Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication) — optional
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics) — opt-in only

Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract) for core app functions, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) for optional cloud features, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest) for minimal crash diagnostics.

International transfers

When you use optional cloud features, data may be transferred to providers in the USA (Groq, Google, Microsoft). We rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses to safeguard your data.

Retention

Local data: as long as you keep the app installed. Cloud backup: until you delete it from Google Drive. Cloud transcription: processed in real time and not retained by the provider. You can delete all data from the app at any time under Settings → Delete account.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to: access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), object (Art. 21), withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)), and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77). To exercise these rights, email dev.app.support@gmail.com.

Users outside the EU have equivalent rights under their local laws (e.g. CCPA/CPRA for California, UK GDPR for the United Kingdom, LGPD for Brazil, PIPL for China, APP for Australia). See the full English Privacy Policy for jurisdiction-specific details.
